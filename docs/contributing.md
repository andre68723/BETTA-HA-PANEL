# Contributing

You are welcome to fork BETTA HA Panel and adapt it to your own setup. This fork
started the same way: by building on the upstream project for the maintainer's
own displays and Home Assistant setup. Please follow the project's non-commercial
[license](https://github.com/andre68723/BETTA-HA-PANEL/blob/main/LICENSE), keep the
copyright/license notices and mark your changes.

Found something wrong or have an idea? [Open an issue](https://github.com/andre68723/BETTA-HA-PANEL/issues).
For bugs, include your display device, firmware version, steps to reproduce and
what you expected. Screenshots or relevant logs help; remove credentials and
private information before sharing.

[Pull requests](https://github.com/andre68723/BETTA-HA-PANEL/pulls) are welcome,
including fixes, features, documentation and translations. Keep each change
focused, explain its purpose and mention what you checked. The
[development guide](development.md) covers firmware checks;
[documentation maintenance](documentation.md) covers the uv/Zensical build.

The original BETTA OS logo is kept unchanged, with [image attribution](img/brand/README.md).
